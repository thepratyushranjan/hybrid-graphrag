# Ganga Infra Holdings — Annual Operations Report 2025

*Fictional sample document created for the Hybrid GraphRAG demo. All companies and people are invented.*

## 1. Company Overview

Ganga Infra Holdings Ltd is an infrastructure group headquartered in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. The group is led by Chief Executive Officer Anjali Mehra and Chief Financial Officer Rohit Verma. In 2025 the group operated three subsidiaries:

- **Ganga Roadways Pvt Ltd**, based in Lucknow, builds and maintains state highways. Managing Director: Vikram Singh.
- **Ganga Smart Power Ltd**, based in Kanpur, installs smart electricity meters and street-light controllers. Managing Director: Neha Kapoor.
- **Ganga Water Systems Ltd**, based in Prayagraj, runs water treatment plants for municipal corporations. Managing Director: Arif Khan.

Group revenue for 2025 was ₹1,240 crore, up 14% on 2024. Ganga Smart Power was the fastest-growing subsidiary, with revenue up 31% after winning the Kanpur Smart Meter Programme.

## 2. Key Vendors and Suppliers

The group works with a small set of strategic vendors. Each vendor is contracted by a specific subsidiary under the Group Master Supply Agreement (MSA-2023).

| Vendor | Supplies | Contracted by | Vendor location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shakti Steel Works | Structural steel, guard rails | Ganga Roadways Pvt Ltd | Kanpur |
| NovaGrid Electronics | Smart meters, meter firmware | Ganga Smart Power Ltd | Noida |
| DataSecure Cloud Pvt Ltd | Cloud hosting of meter and customer data | Ganga Smart Power Ltd | Bengaluru |
| AquaPure Filters | Membrane filters, chemicals | Ganga Water Systems Ltd | Prayagraj |
| Brightline Logistics | Heavy transport | Ganga Roadways Pvt Ltd and Ganga Water Systems Ltd | Lucknow |

NovaGrid Electronics subcontracts its meter-data analytics to DataSecure Cloud Pvt Ltd. This means customer consumption data collected by Ganga Smart Power is processed by two vendors.

## 3. Major Projects in 2025

**Lucknow–Sitapur Highway Upgrade.** Ganga Roadways widened 62 km of the Lucknow–Sitapur state highway. Shakti Steel Works supplied 4,800 tonnes of guard rails. The project finished in November 2025, three weeks ahead of schedule.

**Kanpur Smart Meter Programme.** Ganga Smart Power installed 2.1 lakh smart meters across Kanpur for the Kanpur Electricity Supply Company. Meters are made by NovaGrid Electronics, and all meter readings are stored on DataSecure Cloud servers.

**Prayagraj Water Treatment Plant 3.** Ganga Water Systems commissioned a 120 MLD treatment plant in Prayagraj using AquaPure membrane filters. The plant serves about 9 lakh residents.

## 4. वार्षिक सुरक्षा और अनुपालन सारांश

वर्ष 2025 में गंगा इंफ्रा होल्डिंग्स ने सभी परियोजना स्थलों पर सुरक्षा ऑडिट कराया। ऑडिट का नेतृत्व समूह सुरक्षा प्रमुख सुनीता यादव ने किया।

लखनऊ–सीतापुर राजमार्ग परियोजना में अक्टूबर 2025 में एक दुर्घटना हुई, जिसमें शक्ति स्टील वर्क्स द्वारा आपूर्ति की गई एक गार्ड रेल लगाते समय दो मज़दूर घायल हुए। जाँच में पाया गया कि गार्ड रेल के साथ आवश्यक सुरक्षा प्रमाणपत्र नहीं था। इसके बाद गंगा रोडवेज़ ने शक्ति स्टील वर्क्स से सभी नई खेपों के लिए प्रमाणपत्र अनिवार्य कर दिया।

कानपुर स्मार्ट मीटर कार्यक्रम में ग्राहकों के डेटा की सुरक्षा सबसे बड़ा जोखिम माना गया है, क्योंकि यह डेटा नोवाग्रिड इलेक्ट्रॉनिक्स और डेटासिक्योर क्लाउड दोनों के पास जाता है।

## 5. Risks and Outlook for 2026

The board identified three main risks for 2026:

1. **Data residency.** New state rules may require that customer data stays on servers located in India. DataSecure Cloud currently uses a backup site in Singapore for disaster recovery.
2. **Steel price volatility.** Ganga Roadways depends on a single steel vendor, Shakti Steel Works.
3. **Water quality norms.** Stricter discharge norms could raise chemical costs for Ganga Water Systems.

Rohit Verma has asked every subsidiary to review its vendor contracts against the Q3 2025 Vendor Compliance Bulletin before March 2026.
